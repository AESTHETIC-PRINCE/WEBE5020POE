Our approach

How we work

Confidential risk assessment

We start by listening: your routine, locations, visibility and concerns. A short, confidential assessment shows where the real risks are, so you only pay for protection you need. Book one through our consultation enquiry.

A plan built around you

Personnel, drones, vehicles and routes are matched to the assessment. You receive a clear written plan and indicative budget before anything is deployed.

Discreet delivery

Briefed officers, drivers and pilots work from one operations desk, adjusting to events as they unfold, and report back after every detail.

Target audience

Private clients, celebrities and executives, business owners, event organisers and organisations that require additional security support. Not sure which service fits? Start on our services page.
